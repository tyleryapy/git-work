What to cover

Three sections only. Stay high-level and decision-ready.

# Agenda item Focus
1 H1 initiatives & learnings Key initiatives from H1 — what worked, what didn’t, stop / change / start next year
2 OKR achievement recap Brief status vs. the key OKRs each person set at the start of the year
3 Phase 1 & Phase 2 ideas High-level planning ideas — optimize what we have, then where incremental investment unlocks more

Content expectations

Use this structure for your department deck.

1 · H1 & learnings

Key initiatives from the first half of the year.

  • What worked
  • What did not
  • What to stop
  • What to change
  • What to start next year

2 · OKR recap

Brief achievement recap of the key OKRs provided at the beginning of the year.

  • Hit / miss / on track
  • One line of insight per OKR — not a full narrative

3 · Phase planning

Phase 1

Optimize existing resources to achieve higher results.

Phase 2

High-value additional incremental investment the team wants to ask the company for to achieve higher results.

* Share high-level ideas only for Phase 1 and Phase 2 — not a full business case.
