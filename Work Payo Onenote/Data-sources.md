Hi Yoav,
Thank you very much for reaching out, would like to share the details to keep full transparency as always.

  - **Supplier Payment Revenue – adding revenue not captured by DWH:**This part of revenue is generated from batch payment to suppliers through mass payout platform. However, the revenue is not captured in Revenue fact table in DWH. We need to manually add the revenue for frontline.
  - **Checkout Adjustment – adding revenue prior to FTL:**The sales dashboard reflects revenue occurring after the First-Time Loading date. However, Checkout is different due to the nature of the business: revenue is generated almost immediately after the buyer places an order on the webstore, while volume typically takes time to load into Payoneer (T+3) through card organization→Stripe→Payoneer. Therefore, we need to manually add revenue generated prior to the First-Time Loading date
  - **Special Approval – Win Back Checkout Customers:**We encourage frontline teams to re-engage churned customers with the new checkout solution. If a Checkout BD successfully wins back an existing client (not new customers) using the new solution, the associated revenue will be credited to the frontline team
  - **Special Approval – adding revenue by opport:**This addresses some cases that frontline was unable to create or change the opportunity to “Won” in time due to technical issues.
  - **Hidden Whale Incremental:**  This is a country level initiative, launched in August, primarily targeting customers from **existing unmanaged pool** rather than 2026 new customers. The revenue attribution is calculated using a different logic: the baseline is 12-month average prior to being managed. Once monthly volume exceeds $250K plus the baseline, the hidden whale is considered converted, and frontline teams receive revenue credit for the actual incremental revenue.
  - **B2B New Solution Accelerator:**For every dollar of revenue generated by the B2B new solution, we apply a 4x multiplier. Therefore we add an additional 3x revenue in the manual adjustment column to reach the total 4x value
